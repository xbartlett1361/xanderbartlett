Aether · Growth & GTM lead, contract · Pilot: August 17 to September 21, 2026

We couldn't market Aether until we could explain it.

Aether runs AI coding agents in isolated cloud devboxes, so a developer can run a fleet in parallel and supervise outcomes instead of babysitting terminals. 01 · Sequencing

Why I paused outbound before it started.

Original planEverything at once.

Events, GitHub, Reddit and social, all launched together.

What stopped itFriends wouldn't sign up.

Not even free. Not a rejection: they couldn't tell what it replaced.

The riskOne credible entry.

Burning a developer community on a pitch that fails first contact is worse than never entering.

New orderPosition, distribute, then outbound.

Outbound is now unblocked, aimed at a message that survives first contact.

02 · Positioning & diagnosis

Three findings from user calls, interviews and DMs.

Finding 1

"Are you replacing my CLI?"

The most damaging confusion and the default read. It nearly ended a call with a senior DevOps engineer, until Aether was framed as an augment. Every asset now pre-empts it in the first ten seconds.

Finding 2

One durable differentiator.

Worktrees give source isolation. They don't give runtime isolation: agents still fight over one port, one pool of RAM. Generation parallelizes; verification queues. A machine per agent is what breaks that.

Finding 3

The value stack is real, but secondary.

Scheduled tasks, PR review, looping, environment config: individually replaceable, collectively meaningful. A reason to stay, not a reason to switch.

Turn your AI subscription into a dev team.

The through-line that came out of the diagnosis. The landing page went from a feature wall to this single proposition, and every asset since is built to make it legible in ten seconds.

03 · Channel strategy

Why social leads at Remira and supports at Aether.

At Remira

The buyer gathers on social and buys on trust.

A consumer health app with no budget can't fund paid CAC, and patients trust a person on camera over any ad. So the founder became the channel, and the content earned the right to DM.

At Aether

The buyer buys on proof from peers.

A developer doesn't switch tools because a brand's feed is clever. They switch because someone they trust shows it working. So creators, X, ecosystem listings and comparison pages lead, and owned social compounds underneath.

04 · GTM engineering

The funnel audit moved the constraint.

I audited the full funnel in PostHog. Two findings changed the plan, and three instrumentation gaps limited what any channel report could prove, so fixing them became item one of the next phase.

Signup is not the problem

Known-origin visitors convert at 90%+.

Whatever is wrong, it isn't that qualified visitors refuse to sign up. Cold social traffic views one page and leaves, so the next lever is the landing experience and activation.

Attribution is mostly blind

Instagram strips referrers.

Its in-app browser drops first visits into "direct", hiding the largest social channel. The fix: UTM every outbound link and add a self-reported source question at signup, the cheapest high-value data there is.

Identity isn't stitched

Activation numbers are directional.

Server-side events weren't linked to the browser identity that signed up. Specced the fix with engineering, plus a written definition of activation so every report means the same thing.

05 · Phase 2, now running

What I committed to, in writing.

First two weeks

Measurement first.

Identity stitching and attribution shipped, with a baseline agreed in writing. Contrast assets built from real telemetry. Ecosystem listings and comparison pages shipped.

Ongoing, 90 days

Run the machine.

Weekly user interviews with written synthesis. Organic content across Instagram, X and a rebuilt TikTok. Sponsorships managed end to end, with one long-term creator partner over one-off buys.

Held to

10,000 followers in 90 days.

From ~1,300, organic, on a developer tool. Outcome metrics alongside it: signups by channel, activation rate of attributed signups, and blended CAC across paid placements. Monthly report with kill and double-down calls.

The principle I proposed

The founder should be making product and technical calls. Everything else, I can own.

  • Creator sponsorships end to end: sourcing, negotiation, contracts, payment, conversion analysis
  • The founder's X account: drafted in the founder's voice and approved before anything ships, so the #4 referrer doesn't go quiet during build sprints
  • Site, comparison pages and marketing docs, plus community presence on Reddit, HN, GitHub and Discord
  • User research: recruiting, scheduling, running and synthesizing interviews
  • Partnerships, press and launches, filtered so they reach the founder only when there's a decision to make

The pilot also ended with an open question about which customer to build the marketing engine for. I brought a view, with the market and user evidence behind it, and asked for the decision as an equal discussion. Hedging across both would have been the expensive answer.

Case studyRemira launch

Remira T1D · Co-founder & CEO · Launch, 2026

When you can't buy reach, earn it.

Remira is a pattern-recognition app for Type 1 diabetes. We launched with no budget, no audience and no name into a category of about ten million people worldwide. Every GTM choice followed from three constraints: the money, the market, and how this buyer decides who to trust.

01 · Why social was the strategy

Three constraints, one answer.

The money

Paid CAC was never on the table.

A consumer health app with a free core product can't pay to acquire users and survive. Organic had to be the engine, not the experiment.

The buyer

Patients trust people, not ads.

Nobody comes to their health looking for something trendy. A founder with T1D on camera, talking about the problem they share, earns trust no budget buys. 80% of videos never show the product.

The market

A small, fragmented pond.

About 10M people worldwide, scattered across feeds. TikTok's algorithm finds them for you; a paid campaign has to guess.

04 · Measurement

One north star, held.
